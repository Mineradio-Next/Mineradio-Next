# Mineradio Next Brand Identity

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Mineradio Next uses a professionally proportioned standard eighth note (`♪`) on a deep graphite Windows tile. It must look like a familiar musical glyph before any brand styling is noticed. The identity comes from careful proportion, soft terminals, optical balance, and restrained exterior color echoes rather than abstract deformation.

## 2. Color

- Deep graphite: `#071013`
- Graphite lift: `#152229`
- Frost white: `#F4F8F7`
- Mineral mint: `#55DDB5`
- Ice cyan: `#58C9E8`
- The note face is always solid frost white with no internal color, cutout, stripe, gradient, or seam.
- Mint and cyan appear only as narrow exterior shadows behind the complete note.
- Monochrome versions use only the complete eighth-note silhouette.

## 3. Typography

- Keep the accepted `Mineradio Next` wordmark treatment in the product shell.
- The symbol and wordmark are separate assets; lettering never appears inside the symbol.
- Product wordmark: compact contemporary sans, medium weight, normal letter spacing.
- Technical captions may use Cascadia Mono only in presentation material.

## 4. Spacing & Grid

- Master symbol uses a 16-unit construction grid.
- The note head is an optically enlarged tilted ellipse at approximately 1.30:1. Its visible area is 18-22% larger than a neutral text-glyph construction, while remaining clearly elliptical rather than teardrop-shaped.
- The upper-right shoulder of the note head overlaps the stem base to read as one continuous silhouette. The stem is vertical and approximately 14% of symbol width.
- The flag descends naturally from the top of the stem and occupies approximately 31% of total symbol width.
- Optical balance takes priority over mathematical centering. The symbol sits slightly left and low inside the tile.
- At 16 px, exterior shadows disappear and the frost-white silhouette remains.

## 5. Layout & Composition

- The bare symbol has no permanent ring, disc, diamond, or badge.
- Windows app assets place it on a neutral deep-graphite squircle; the squircle is packaging rather than logo geometry.
- The standard construction remains readable: elliptical note head, straight vertical stem, and one downward-curving flag.
- Exterior color exposure remains below 5% of symbol width.

## 6. Components

- Master symbol: one standard eighth note with a full tilted elliptical head, integrated head-to-stem shoulder, straight stem, and single descending flag.
- Color shadows: one mineral-mint rear edge and one ice-cyan rear edge, visible only outside the frost-white note.
- Small-size symbol: full-color at 32 px and above; simplified single mint edge at 24 px; monochrome at 16 px.
- Windows family: 512 px PNG, multi-size ICO, taskbar icon, monochrome tray mark, installer header, installer sidebar, and startup mark.

## 7. Motion & Interaction

- Startup motion reveals the two exterior shadows first, then the white eighth note settles over them in 540-680 ms.
- Hover may separate the shadows by 1 px and return them; it must not loop.
- No bounce, spin, orbit, equalizer motion, RGB glitch, rotating disc, or permanent glow.
- Reduced-motion mode renders the complete static mark immediately.

## 8. Voice & Brand

The identity is clear, musical, calm, and independent. It should feel like a serious Windows player, not an experimental symbol, commercial streaming clone, or generic media utility.

## 9. Anti-patterns

- No distorted, S-shaped, ribbon-like, hook-like, handwritten, or abstract note.
- No color inside the note body.
- No play triangle, pause bars, transport glyphs, `MN`, person, mascot, or diamond.
- No radio arcs, broadcast waves, orbit lines, double tail, stacked tail, detached tail, or decorative appendage.
- No Douyin black face, cyan-red pairing, circular head ratio, or copied flag curvature.
- No QQ Music yellow disc and green-blue note; no NetEase Cloud Music red tile or spiral.
- Color must not repair a weak silhouette. The monochrome eighth note must remain balanced.
