# Mineradio Next Identity Handoff

Read `docs/brand/DESIGN.md` and `docs/brand/design-contract.md` before exporting assets.

Use a standard eighth note (`♪`) with:

- an optically enlarged tilted elliptical note head, with 18-22% more visible area than a neutral text-glyph construction;
- a continuous upper-right shoulder overlapping the stem base, with no notch or detached joint;
- a straight vertical stem;
- one conventional downward-curving flag;
- a completely solid frost-white face with no internal color;
- one narrow mineral-mint rear shadow and one narrow ice-cyan rear shadow, visible only outside the white note;
- a deep graphite Windows squircle;
- no letters, play controls, discs, diamonds, broadcast arcs, internal patches, abstract curves, or borrowed brand proportions.
- no circular or teardrop note head derived from QQ Music; preserve the original approximately 1.30:1 ellipse.

Prove the mark at 512, 128, 48, 32, 24, and 16 px. At 16 px, use the white silhouette alone. Keep exterior color exposure below 5% of symbol width and place the symbol optically left and low within the tile.

Do not replace production icon, titlebar, startup, tray, or installer assets before user approval.
