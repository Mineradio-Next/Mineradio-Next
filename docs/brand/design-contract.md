# Mineradio Next Logo Design Contract

## Goal

Create an original Windows logo family for Mineradio Next using a standard, immediately recognizable eighth note. The audience is desktop music listeners. The current deliverable is a high-fidelity structural preview; production assets remain unchanged until approval.

## Evidence

| Evidence | Confidence | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User feedback across thirteen logo rounds | provided | The mark must be a normal eighth note with a visually full head, not an invented hook, ribbon, S-curve, monogram, play symbol, or abstract gesture. |
| User-provided NetEase Cloud Music, Douyin, and QQ Music desktop screenshots | provided | Prefer immediate music recognition, a stable app-tile silhouette, and strong small-size behavior. |
| Douyin official horizontal SVG sourced from ByteDance | observed | A complete primary glyph and restrained exterior color separation are effective, but its exact geometry and cyan-red system must not be reused. |
| Current Mineradio Next shell | observed | Deep neutral surfaces, frost white, mineral mint, and ice cyan fit the existing product. |

## Reference Boundaries

| Reference | Keep | Change | Do not copy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Douyin | complete foreground glyph; restrained exterior color layering; strong 16 px read | use a conventional eighth note, tilted elliptical head, frost-white face, mineral palette, and different offsets | black face, cyan-red pairing, circular head, exact stem/flag proportions and curvature |
| QQ Music | familiar music notation and stable mass | remove the disc and use a graphite tile with a white note | yellow disc, green-blue note, circular composition |
| NetEase Cloud Music | high contrast and desktop recognizability | use a filled eighth note rather than a spiral line mark | red tile, cloud-disc spiral, white line construction |

## Final Stance

The mark is a standard eighth note with an optically enlarged tilted elliptical head, straight vertical stem, and one naturally descending flag. The head gains 18-22% visible area and joins the stem through a continuous upper-right shoulder, but stays elliptical and does not reproduce QQ Music's teardrop construction. The front face is uninterrupted frost white. Narrow mineral-mint and ice-cyan shadows sit behind it and appear only outside its boundary. Originality comes from the measured ellipse ratio, integrated shoulder, rounded terminal treatment, compact flag, graphite tile, mineral palette, and Windows-specific small-size drawings, not from deforming the musical symbol.

## Risks And Unknowns

- The eighth note is a common category symbol, so final public release still needs trademark similarity review.
- Exterior shadows must remain subtle enough to avoid a glitch effect.
- At 16 px the shadows are removed to protect glyph clarity.
- Installer composition is deferred until the symbol is approved.

## Quality Gate

- Reads immediately as a normal `♪` at every size.
- Uses a straight stem and one conventional descending flag.
- The head feels full at taskbar size without becoming circular or teardrop-shaped.
- The white face contains no internal color or cutout.
- Monochrome silhouette works without shadows.
- Does not reproduce the references' silhouettes, proportions, or color systems.
